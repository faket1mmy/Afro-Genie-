Our Story

Two Attorneys. One Mission. Twenty-Seven Years Later.

Sterling & Hale was founded in 1998 by Margaret Sterling and James Hale on a simple belief: every client deserves the same quality of representation, regardless of the size of their case or their ability to pay a premium retainer.

What began as a two-person practice out of a small downtown office has grown into a respected twelve-attorney firm serving individuals, families, and businesses across the region — while holding onto the personal, client-first culture that defined our first case.

  • Founded in 1998 by Margaret Sterling & James Hale
  • 12 attorneys, 6 practice areas, 1 shared standard of care
  • AV Preeminent® rated by Martindale-Hubbell
3,200+ Cases successfully resolved for our clients
What Drives Us

Our Mission & Core Values

Our mission is to provide accessible, ethical, and effective legal representation that protects our clients' rights and secures their futures.

  Integrity

We give honest assessments of every case — including the hard truths — because trust is the foundation of good counsel.

  Compassion

Legal matters are often the hardest moments in our clients' lives. We listen first, and we never lose sight of the person behind the case.

  Diligence

No detail is too small. We prepare every case as though it will go to trial, even when it doesn't.

  Accessibility

Flexible fee structures, evening appointments, and multilingual staff ensure quality counsel is within reach.

  Excellence

Our attorneys pursue ongoing legal education and specialty certifications to stay ahead of an evolving legal landscape.

  Confidentiality

Your case details, records, and communications are protected with the strictest standards of client confidentiality.
